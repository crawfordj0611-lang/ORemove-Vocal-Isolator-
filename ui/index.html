Welcome to ORemove

Clean vocals and instrumentals from any song, right on your computer.

  1. Add a song. Drop audio files on the window, browse for them, or paste a YouTube link.
  2. Pick a model. The default, BS-Roformer 1297, is the best all-rounder; MelBand Roformer gives the fullest results.
  3. Separate. You get a vocal and an instrumental stem to play, compare and drag into your DAW.
